Simple Track

A calorie and nutrition log for iPhone that keeps your food diary on your phone.

Privacy Policy Support

Simple Track records what you eat, works out what your body actually seems to burn, and adjusts your daily calorie target as the weeks go by. It does that without an account, a subscription, or a server.

What it does

  • Log a repeat food in two taps from the Today screen.
  • Search 13,600 foods from USDA FoodData Central, bundled in the app and available offline.
  • Scan a barcode, or read a nutrition label with the camera.
  • Build recipes with a measured cooked weight, so a weighed plate is logged accurately.
  • Watch your weight trend and let your calorie target follow it.
  • Share a meal with someone as a file, or export your whole history, encrypted.

What it does not do

No account. No sign-in. No subscription. No ads. No analytics. No tracking. Your food diary is on your phone, and Simple Track has no server to send it to.

A note on what this is

Simple Track is a general wellness tool, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, or prescribe. If you are managing a health condition, are pregnant, have a history of disordered eating, or are setting targets for a child, please talk to a qualified professional.
