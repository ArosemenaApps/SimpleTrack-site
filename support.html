Common questions

Why does a day show a dash instead of a number?

Because that nutrient is genuinely unknown, not zero. A calories-only entry records calories and nothing else, so the day's protein is unknown rather than pretending to be zero. The app shows a dash instead of a number it cannot stand behind.

Why does it say "Not enough data" for my daily burn?

Working out what you actually burn needs a stretch of days you have finished logging and a run of weigh-ins around both ends of that stretch. The Progress screen names whichever requirement is still missing. Until then the app uses a starting estimate based on your height, age, and activity.

Can I set my own calorie target?

Yes. Settings → Targets and goal → Set target manually. A manual target stays exactly where you put it. The app keeps working out what it would recommend and shows that separately, but never replaces your number with it.

The app scanned a barcode it does not know.

Open Food Facts is contributed to by the public, so newer or local products are often missing. The app offers to create the food from the pack, and remembers the barcode against it, so the next scan finds it instantly and offline.

I read a restaurant label and the calories look wrong.

Restaurant screens often show one calorie figure in the header and a different one in the nutrition panel — the panel is rounded for labelling, and the header may reflect a customised order. When the two disagree, the app shows both and asks you to pick. Add-on calories for extras are never counted, and the review screen says so.

How do I move to a new iPhone?

Restoring the new phone from your normal iPhone backup brings Simple Track's history with it. For a belt-and-braces copy, Settings → Export everything writes an encrypted file you can keep anywhere. Keep the password: there is no way to recover it.

How do I delete everything?

Settings → Delete all Simple Track data on this iPhone. Anything written to Apple Health is removed separately, with a control that touches only the entries this app wrote.
